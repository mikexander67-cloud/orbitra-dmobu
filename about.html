About Orbitra

We help small Mauritian businesses launch online — properly.

Orbitra builds the digital foundations a business needs to be visible and credible: website, branding, and social presence. Delivered as defined packages with payments spread over two to three months — not open-ended custom projects.

Why we exist

A structured middle ground — between a freelancer and a full agency.

When a small business in Mauritius wants to get online, the choices usually come down to two. Hire a freelancer — affordable, but ad-hoc, with work delivered one piece at a time and little support once it's done. Or approach a full agency — capable, but expensive and often out of reach for a business that's just starting out.

Orbitra sits deliberately between the two. We sell launch readiness: the website, branding, and first social presence a business needs to open its doors online, bundled into a clear package with a fixed price and a defined timeline.

Just as important as what we build is making sure you can run it. Every package includes training calls, a written manual, and a window of WhatsApp support — so when we hand over, you're not left guessing.

And because we know cash flow matters most at the start, every package is paid as a deposit plus installments over two to three months, via MCB Juice or bank transfer. No big lump-sum cheque to launch.

Honest fit

When Orbitra might not be the right choice.

We'd rather tell you upfront than take on work that won't serve you. Orbitra probably isn't the right fit if:

  • You need a large, fully bespoke platform with custom functionality — a full agency will serve you better.
  • You want the lowest possible price above everything, and you're comfortable managing freelancers task by task.
  • You already have a strong brand and website and only need one small, isolated piece of work.
  • You'd prefer to stay hands-off entirely — our process includes a short audit and a few calls, because the finished site is yours to run.

If any of these sound like you, we're glad to point you toward a better option. And if your business grows into a bigger launch down the line, the door stays open.
