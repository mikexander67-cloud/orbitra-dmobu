Payments & installments

How paying for your launch works.

How do I pay Orbitra?

Two simple ways: MCB Juice (send-to-mobile) or a direct bank transfer to our Mauritian business account. Those are the only methods we use to collect payment.

Can I pay in installments?

Yes — that's the whole idea. Every package is a deposit followed by installments, spread over two to three months. The exact split is listed on each package, so you know it before you commit.

For example, the Full Launch is a Rs 26,000 deposit plus two installments of Rs 19,500 — never one large lump sum.

Is my deposit refundable?

It's refundable within 7 days of signing, as long as work hasn't started. Once discovery begins, the deposit becomes non-refundable, and any installment paid against a completed milestone is non-refundable too.

When do I get payment instructions?

They're included in your scope document at signing — so you'll have everything you need, in writing, before any money changes hands.

What about Stripe, MIPS, or PayPal?

Those are payment options we set up on the ecommerce stores we build — so your customers can pay you at checkout. They're never how you pay Orbitra. You pay us by MCB Juice or bank transfer.

Scope & delivery

What's included, and how it's built.

What exactly is included in a package?

Each package has a fixed, written scope — the website, branding, and social deliverables are all listed in full. No surprise quotes and no scope creep. You can see every package and what it includes on the Packages page.

What platform will my site be built on?

It depends on the package:

  • Starter Launch — a fast static site hosted on Vercel
  • Essential, Full and Premium Launch — WordPress, hosted on Teal Host
  • Ecommerce packages — Shopify

We'll confirm the right fit for you at the scope-review stage.

How long does a launch take?

Each package has a defined timeline, from 30 days for a Starter Launch up to 75–90 days for our largest builds. Delivery is milestone-based, so you review the work as it progresses rather than waiting until the very end.

What if I need more than the package includes?

You can add extras at the same fixed-price approach — add-ons, upgrade paths, and upsell packages are all listed with clear prices. Add them at the scope-review stage, or any time after you launch.

Do I own my website once it's done?

Yes, completely. At handover you receive full admin access, plus hosting and domain credentials in your own name, along with a manual and recordings of your training calls. The site is yours to keep and run.

Care Plan

Keeping your site running after launch.

What is the Care Plan?

It's an optional, strongly recommended monthly plan that keeps your site live and healthy — hosting, domain and business email active, daily backups, security monitoring, up to two minor content updates a month, and a monthly performance snapshot.

Do I have to take the Care Plan?

No. If you decline, you're choosing to self-manage — and we hand over everything you need to do that: full admin access, hosting and domain credentials in your name, a step-by-step manual, and recordings of your training calls.

Just know that without an active Care Plan, Orbitra won't make updates on your behalf, monitor uptime, handle renewals, or restore your site if something breaks. You can subscribe at any time later, with no penalty for skipping it upfront.

How much does it cost?

There are four commitment tiers — the longer the commitment, the lower the effective monthly rate:

  • Monthly — Rs 3,500/month, no commitment
  • Quarterly — Rs 9,500/quarter (≈ Rs 3,167/month, ~10% off)
  • Semi-annual — Rs 17,500/6 months (≈ Rs 2,917/month, ~17% off)
  • Annual — Rs 32,000/year (≈ Rs 2,667/month, ~24% off)

If you bundle the first year with your launch package, the annual plan is Rs 28,000 instead of Rs 32,000 — a one-time saving of Rs 4,000 at point of launch.

What if I need an extra update or some help?

Your plan includes up to two minor updates each month. Beyond that, additional updates are Rs 1,500 each, billed transparently. If you're not on a Care Plan, one-off support is available at Rs 1,500/hour, with a 30-minute minimum block.
